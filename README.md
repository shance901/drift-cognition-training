## Small Business Grants & Funding Database

See [`database/`](database/) for a SQLite database identifying grant and funding
programs for small businesses in cybersecurity, cybersecurity protection/compliance,
and general small business funding. Includes a build script, a search/export CLI,
and human-editable seed data — see [`database/README.md`](database/README.md) for
usage.
